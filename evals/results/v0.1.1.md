# Version 0.1.1 evaluation record

This maintenance update narrows context inspection and completion boundaries, and clarifies normalization/filtering/aggregation order. Engineering, scientific and authorization checks remain distinct.

## Scope and method

Two fabricated cases were added: an existing metric definition and a review of prior-check reuse. The maintainer walked through the requested behavior and executed only the metric fixture's arithmetic. This is a manual scenario review, not a blind independent model run, a latency/token benchmark, or evidence of scientific validity.

The initial three model forward-runs remain historical v0.1.0 evidence in [their original record](v0.1.0.md); they were not rerun or relabeled for this patch. Installer code is unchanged. Package validation and the existing installer suite remain the repository's required CI checks.

## Expected and reviewed outcomes

- Bounded definition: filter eligible units, calculate their percentages 20, 10 and 60, then take the median, 20. Pooling produces 23.75 and averaging percentages produces 30; neither is the implemented median. The ineligible unit does not contribute. Answering this definition requires no project scaffold or cluster access.
- Documentation-only handoff: reuse still-bound implementation/data checks, check the changed wording against the definition, and finish the requested review. No new approval is needed for the recorded authorized action.
- Changed-input handoff: prior implementation checks can remain relevant, but the old data-dependent result does not validate the new input. Name the affected input/result checks without starting an unauthorized analysis or rerunning unrelated work.

The package includes no private task excerpts, actual study records or machine-specific research paths. Primary upstream revisions and the decision not to add dependencies are recorded in [the maintenance record](v0.1.1.json).

## Limits

The review verifies the intended scope and the worked example. It does not measure how reliably other agents will follow the revised instruction or establish a reduction in runtime, latency or cost. Real research continues to require its own appropriate evidence and executable validation.
