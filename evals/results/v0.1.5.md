# Version 0.1.5 evaluation record

This patch makes the scope of statistical calibration explicit: the evidence must apply to the target estimator, interval or decision, with the relevant labels, dependence and analysis steps. It retains valid engineering checks and the preceding paper/code discovery workflow.

## Validation scope

The maintainer reviewed the fabricated [calibration-scope case](../cases/calibration-scope/TASK.md) and executed its deterministic arithmetic. This is a manual scenario assessment and mathematical counterexample, not a blind agent evaluation or an empirical calibration study.

The aligned and balanced constructions have exactly the same 32 component values and zero overall means. Both have 16 component values beyond the arbitrary absolute threshold of 1.75. Their eight row means cross that threshold four times and zero times, respectively. Thus the supplied marginal diagnostics do not determine the aggregate behavior. These counts are not estimates of real type-I error, power or interval coverage.

The archived evidence concerns a different label, independent rows and a component score. It does not establish calibration of the revised final contrast with repeated/related observations. The review preserves unchanged schema/row checks and unaffected transform tests. The next proposed check targets the final estimator and decision, relevant null/weak regimes, dependence, fit failures and simulation uncertainty. No original research or cluster job was run.

The public methods source is [Morris, White and Crowther (2019)](https://doi.org/10.1002/sim.8086), sections 3 and 5. The publisher full text was inspected; the initial PMC endpoint presented a browser check. No upstream implementation was copied. Pinned upstream observations, executed arithmetic and runtime fingerprints are recorded in [v0.1.5.json](v0.1.5.json).

## Software checks and limits

The applicable software checks are package/link validation, the official skill validator and the existing 23 isolated installer tests. Their results are recorded separately from scenario interpretation. Installer code, invocation policy and personal settings are unchanged.

The example shows one reason to match evidence to the final inference. It does not certify any real analysis, demonstrate autonomous adherence to the guidance or require simulations for every research task. New checks remain conditional on the current question, evidence gap and authorization. Earlier forward-runs remain scoped to [v0.1.0](v0.1.0.md); the preceding discovery update is documented in [v0.1.4](v0.1.4.md).
